# Issue #130 independently reviewed selected rights

[Per-field rights](rights.json) identify each exact source/span,
attribution, modification, applicable original-work layer and independent decision.

## Source layers

The 50 fixed French Wiktionnaire captures retain full oldid/history links,
the complete paginated named contributor registry and anonymous counts in
[source context](source-context.json). Seven responses complete the contributor
chain. These are article-level credits; they do not identify a sentence author.
Fixed lexical definitions and grammar/IPA notations form the wiki contribution
layer. Lexique's French adaptations and newly written EN/ZH explanations form
the adaptation layer and retain CC BY-SA 4.0.

Twenty-five articles have DAF8 import notices. Original credit remains
**Académie française, Dictionnaire de l’Académie française, eighth edition,
1932–1935**. The existing independently reviewed
[#129 original-work analysis](../issue-129/selected-rights.md) records the
institutional collective-work basis under CPI L123-3: using final publication
1935 gives 1936-01-01 + 70 years = **2006-01-01**. This historical-work basis does
not independently approve any new selected sentence. Later electronic editing
and wiki contributions retain their own rights.

References to Littré identify a consulted work; they do not establish an imported
selected sentence. No Littré import layer is declared in these 50 captures.

`auteur` has CFC markers in unselected cartography definitions at lines 54/56.
Only the marker and its location are retained in tracked reference context.
The selected writer definition at line 30 and nominal IPA at line 16 use none
of that cartography prose. CFC material receives no redistribution admission.
References to TLFi or other works are bibliographic identities, not broad
permissions to copy their contents. Unselected examples, media, etymologies and
other-language prose are excluded from the proposed dictionary content.

The separate IPA source for `la plupart des` is the existing **Lexique 3.83**
CC BY-SA 4.0 dataset by Boris New and Christophe Pallier, with its bundled full
source, provenance and contributor README. Original line 75479 and documented
phonemic-code conversion are retained in [phrase evidence](lexique-phrase-source.json).
This licensed dataset is not public domain. The manual at fixed openlexicon
commit 298010e765c49673317aa5b5c3b068df0351725a is reference evidence for Tables
2/3; no manual page or artwork becomes learner-facing dictionary content.

attribution, modifications, ShareAlike and no additional restrictions. Wikimedia
Terms section 7 preserves the imported-content distinction and permits article/
history attribution. Fixed copyright-policy revision 38903168 and DAF8-template
revision 22113837 are reused with their retained policy captures from #129.

licence and names Boris New, Christophe Pallier, Ronald Peereman, Sophie Dufour,
