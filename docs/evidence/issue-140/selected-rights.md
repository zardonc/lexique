Original expression first published in 1932 or 1935 would, if that term applies, expire no earlier than 2028-01-01 or 2031-01-01 respectively. The article marker does not establish the original sentence/volume, publication and restoration conditions, a permission grant, or a specific basis that the expression/source excerpt is not copyrightable. These are exact missing evidence, not a finding that every phrase is protected or infringes. The two first-party US legal captures, request URLs, dates and bytes are retained in the review archive.
