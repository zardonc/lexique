# Issue #117 selected-text rights review

**All 200 fields and supporting source boundaries pass independent selected-text
rights review.** Actual combined-package compliance remains pending.
[Independent review](independent-review.md) identifies the reviewer and pinned
author/base revisions.

## Selected boundaries

All 50 fixed Wiktionnaire articles were read for selected definitions, French
grammatical forms, IPA, neighboring senses and import/reference markers.
[Context](source-context.json) and [sources](sources.json) record an individual
reason for every article; [rights](rights.json) records each field decision and
exact revision/span/contributor binding. Definitions and new explanatory
translations retain CC BY-SA 4.0. Original proposals and rights assessments
remain alongside the decisions.

Thirty-eight articles contain DAF8 import notices or original-edition references.
Their Académie française eighth-edition 1932–1935 layer retains separate credit
and the established collective-work CPI L123-3 basis. Using final publication
in 1935 gives the conservative expiry of 2006-01-01. The
[#99 basis](../issue-99/source-context.json) and fixed French rights policy were
read. Wiktionnaire transcription/adaptation and Lexique adaptations remain
separately CC BY-SA 4.0. Page markers and bibliography do not establish authorship
of individual selected sentences.

Selected lexical text excludes literary/news quotation prose, etymology,
bilingual tables, other languages and image/audio assets. Short example
fragments supply only conventional construction/form facts; surrounding
expressive prose is not copied or translated. At `moteur` line 97 only the
machine definition after the image syntax supplies adapted wording.
`brave homme` supplies the ordinary kindness collocation, not its literary prose.

## Authorities and redistribution

The archived CC BY-SA 4.0 legal text, Wikimedia Terms section 7 and fixed French
copyright/import authorities were independently read. Policy revision:
**38903168**; DAF8 template: **22113837**; CFC template: **29619964**.
Raw authorities match their saved API content. CFC does not supply batch fields.
Complete primary captures and extracted text remain in the author archive and
reviewer evidence root.

- CC BY-SA 4.0 section 2 permits sharing and adaptations in the selected format,
  including commercial use. Do not imply official endorsement.
- Section 3 requires supplied creator/copyright/licence/disclaimer notices,
  reasonably practicable source links, modification and prior-modification
  notices, and licence text/link. Adaptations retain ShareAlike.
- Section 4 preserves applicable database obligations for extraction/reuse.
  No additional legal restrictions or effective technological measures may
  obstruct licensed rights.
- Wikimedia Terms section 7 recognises article/full-history attribution and
  requires compatible imported rights, original-source notices and
  modification/licensing notices. Media retain separate terms.
- Parent conversion must carry fixed revisions/spans, full contributor and
  original-edition credits, editable source inputs, legal text and actual notices
  into the artifact. Project MIT does not relicense dictionary content.

Eight terminating contributor responses retain complete current registries for
50 articles with **3,335 names summed per article**. Registries can extend beyond
selected revisions; article/full-history links also cover anonymous contributors.
Neither last editors nor registries establish selected-sentence authorship.

## Limits

The review uses retained first-party captures and the established original-edition
basis; no live refetch or statute refresh is claimed. Actual assembled notices,
canonical conversion, packaged content, native execution and release remain
parent-owned gates.
