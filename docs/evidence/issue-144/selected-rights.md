# Selected source rights: #144 author proposal

The 200 proposed fields retain **CC BY-SA 4.0** for fixed Wiktionnaire
contributions and Lexique adaptations. French definitions are scoped
adaptations; English and Chinese are newly authored explanations of the same
selected French semantics. They are not upstream bilingual quotations. IPA
selects one recorded isolated variant and adds slashes. Project code remains MIT.

The [field ledger](rights.json) records each exact source revision, selected
span, attribution, modification and original-work layer. All entries await a
separate selected-text rights decision. The [original proposals](authored/rights.json)
retain those pending decisions; the author assigns no independent acceptance.

Twenty-seven fixed articles contain Académie française eighth-edition import
markers. The original collective dictionary, **1932–1935**, retains separate
credit. The established [original-work rights evidence](../issue-129/selected-rights.md)
applies [CPI L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941/2026-02-20):
seventy years from the January following publication. Using final publication
in 1935 gives **2006-01-01**. That original-work determination does not accept
these new fields or later electronic editorial work. Original author/work
respect remains under [L121-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278891).
Wiktionnaire and Lexique contribution layers retain CC BY-SA 4.0.

Article-level import markers and reference lists do not identify a selected
sentence's author. The full selected lexical spans and their French/POS frames
are retained in [source excerpts](source-excerpts.json). Literary and news
examples, other-language text, etymologies, images and audio supply no field.
The CFC cartographic definition in `filet`, line 93, is explicitly excluded;
the net definition at line 15 supplies its selected meanings. Bibliographic
references to other dictionaries grant no redistribution right to their prose.

`sienne/PRO:pos` uses the fixed `la sienne` possessive-pronoun definition and
agreement table, with the isolated `sienne` IPA. Its required article and
feminine singular agreement remain readable. No bare-pronoun use or runtime
alias is admitted. `four` uses its cooking-compartment definition plus the
fixed `four électrique` cooking sense. `ciao/ONO` uses only the French informal
farewell frame. `go/NOM` selects the two-player board game, excluding the
separately referenced slang girlfriend and verb definitions.

The retained policy revision **38903168**, DAF8-template revision **22113837**,
CC legal text and Wikimedia Terms are listed with their original capture dates
and reuse origins in [rights.json](rights.json). The template says all or part
was extracted and may have changed; it does not prove sentence authorship.
CC BY-SA sections 2(a), 3(a), 3(b) and 4 allow the recorded sharing/adaptations
with attribution, modification notices, ShareAlike and no added restrictions.
Wikimedia Terms section 7 permits linked article/history attribution and
preserves imported material's separate rights.

The [notices](NOTICES.md), [complete legal text](COPYING), fixed article/history
URLs, complete named contributor chains, returned anonymous counts and original
credits define the author package's obligations. Contributor registries may
include later editors; complete linked histories credit anonymous contributors
without claiming sentence-level authorship. Actual combined materials,
conversion, packaged notices and native verification remain integration-owned.
