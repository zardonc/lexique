## Modifications and selected boundaries

One everyday sense is selected per exact group. French definitions adapt the
selected lexical semantics, remove markup and retain the necessary construction
and register. English and Chinese are newly written explanations of that same use.
One attested isolated-headword IPA variant is selected and surrounded by slashes.
No liaison, audio, image, etymology or attributed literary/news example is included
in learner fields. The full raw articles are retained as research evidence only.

`surveiller`, `trahir` and `automobile` provide exact supporting lexical definitions
for `surveillance`, `trahison` and `auto`. They are not additional frozen groups or
lookup aliases. `évanouir/VER` remains the frozen lemma while its selected use is
explicitly `s’évanouir`; the IPA is the lemma’s applicable pronunciation.
