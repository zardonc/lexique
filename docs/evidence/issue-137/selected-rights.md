## Contribution and adaptation layers

The selected French definitions and isolated IPA notations are lexical/phonetic
content from fixed French Wiktionnaire revisions. [Source context](source-context.json)
retains language/POS frames, current article contributors, anonymous counts,
history links, import markers and external bibliographic identities. New Lexique
English/Chinese explanations are scoped to those French definitions, with exact
lexical support where the primary definition refers to another word. They do not
carry an upstream translation author or an independent accuracy decision.

Wiktionnaire contributions, Lexique French adaptations and new EN/ZH explanations
use **CC BY-SA 4.0**. [COPYING](COPYING), sections 2(a), 3(a), 3(b) and 4, supplies
the legal text. Sharing/adaptation requires attribution, licence identification,
modification notices, ShareAlike and no additional restrictions. Project source
code keeps MIT; these selected dictionary fields are not relicensed as MIT.

## Original-work boundary

**32 of the 53 fixed source articles** declare imports from Académie
française’s *Dictionnaire de l’Académie française*, eighth edition, **1932–1935**.
The proposed original-work basis reuses the established
[#99 record](../issue-99/source-context.json) and independently accepted
[#129 boundary](../issue-129/selected-rights.md) for that institutional collective
edition, separate from later electronic editorial work. [CPI L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941/2026-02-20)
provides seventy years from the January following collective publication:
**1936-01-01 + 70 years = 2006-01-01**, using the final 1935 publication.
Original author/work respect remains under [L121-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278891).
Original credits and modification notices therefore remain in [NOTICES](NOTICES.md).
No prior review is reused for the new wording or newly selected spans.

Import notices state that all or part came from DAF8 and may have changed. They
do not prove selected-sentence authorship. Reference markers such as TLFi likewise
do not establish origin or confer reuse rights. The proposed fields use the exact
selected lexical definitions, grammatical frames and isolated IPA, excluding
neighbouring attributed quotations, other-language text, media and etymologies.
Raw full-page API/wiki captures remain research evidence, outside learner content.
