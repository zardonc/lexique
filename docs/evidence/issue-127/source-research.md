feminine advertising /pyb/, not the masculine bar. `bite` retains its vulgar
register; `monseigneur` retains its honorific restrictions. The sourced
`romantique` sense is idealistic, with imagination prevailing over reality.
`sable` retains the source grain-size/material scope. `jeudi` uses a lexical
substring outside the ISO reference. `émotion` uses its headword grammar line
without the illustration. All construction and excluded-use notes remain in
the group and field-review records. No alias or annotation is proposed.
