The four proposed fields, exclusions, modification notices and grammar/form evidence are individually bound in the JSON records. `cents` retains its exact adjective-plural relationship to cardinal `cent`, the no-following-completing-numeral restriction and exclusion of ordinal/currency uses. `vous-même` addresses a single person politely; `grand-chose` retains negation and invariability. No normalization, spelling substitute or runtime alias is proposed.
