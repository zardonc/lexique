## Content and original-work layers

The fixed Wiktionnaire wording/transcription and Lexique’s French adaptations
and new English/Chinese explanations retain
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en).
The [complete legal text](COPYING), sections 2(a), 3(a), 3(b) and 4, permits
sharing, adaptation and database extraction subject to attribution, source and
licence identification, modification notices, ShareAlike and no additional
restrictions. Wikimedia Terms of Use section 7 permits article/history-link
attribution and retains imported material’s separate obligations.

Thirty-four articles declare imports from **Académie française, Dictionnaire
de l’Académie française, eighth edition, 1932–1935**. The independently reviewed
[#129 original-work basis](../issue-129/selected-rights.md) is reused only for
that original institutional collective work. Under
[CPI L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941/2026-02-20),
final publication in 1935 gives 1936-01-01 plus seventy years, conservatively
**2006-01-01**. Attribution and work respect remain under
[L121-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278891).
Later wiki transcription and Lexique adaptations remain CC BY-SA 4.0.
Historical independent review does not accept any new #147 field.

Policy revision **38903168** and DAF8-template revision **22113837** distinguish
external content from the wiki contribution layer. The import template says
all or part may derive from DAF8 and may have changed; it does not establish
the author of a particular selected sentence. TLFi, Littré, DAF8 and other
bibliographic references are retained as context, not licences for external
prose. No modern TLFi, XMLittré, ARTFL or other external transcription is imported.

## Selected boundaries

Every field is bound to the actual French definition or applicable IPA line in
its captured revision. The exact noun, adjective, verb or adverb frame is
retained separately. French definitions are scoped adaptations; English and
Chinese are newly authored explanations of those same selected uses. They
require separate language review. IPA selects one recorded isolated variant,
including nominal `détenu` rather than borrowing its participle pronunciation.

`marge` selects the page border at line 19. Its CFC cartography definition at
line 40 is excluded. `grain` selects cereal seed at line 11; CFC graphic
definitions at lines 89–90 are excluded. CFC prose and rights are not proposed
for redistribution. The short `Semblable au` grammar fragment is recorded at
its exact column in line 10; the surrounding Huysmans quotation is excluded.
Other literary/news example bodies, media, etymologies and other-language
meanings supply no learner field.

The `golf` proposal retains the ball/club/hole/grass-course meaning in the
French frame and omits the captured definition’s erroneous two-camp condition.
`homicide` describes killing a human without adding intentional-murder status.
`prostitué` preserves the male-person restriction. `chèvre` selects the female
animal. `manche` selects the feminine sleeve. `batterie` selects rechargeable
connected accumulators, excluding broader non-rechargeable-cell uses.
`tsar` remains a contemporary word for a historical title, without a specialist
exception or a changed frozen group.
