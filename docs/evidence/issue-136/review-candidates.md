## 2751 — nôtre/PRO:pos

Selected use: First-person plural possessive pronoun in the article construction; retain the circumflex and agreement.

**french_definition:** Pronom possessif de la première personne du pluriel : ce qui est à nous. Il se construit avec un article : le nôtre, la nôtre, les nôtres, selon le genre et le nombre de ce qui est remplacé.

Fixed basis: [le nôtre, revision 38903931](https://fr.wiktionary.org/w/index.php?title=le%20n%C3%B4tre&oldid=38903931), line 10, column 1. The le nôtre possessive-pronoun section and its agreement table support first-person plural possession with le/la/les; the bare nôtre article supplies only the /notʁ/ segment. The circumflex, mandatory article and agreement remain, excluding determiner notre and obsolete independent adjective uses.

**ipa:** /notʁ/

Fixed basis: [nôtre, revision 39607445](https://fr.wiktionary.org/w/index.php?title=n%C3%B4tre&oldid=39607445), line 9, column 1. Bare nôtre line 9 records /notʁ/ and le nôtre line 8 embeds the same segment in all article forms. The IPA is the isolated segment, not unaccented notre /nɔtʁ/ or the full article construction.

**english:** Ours; first-person plural possessive pronoun, used with an article: le nôtre, la nôtre or les nôtres, agreeing with what it replaces.

Fixed basis: [le nôtre, revision 38903931](https://fr.wiktionary.org/w/index.php?title=le%20n%C3%B4tre&oldid=38903931), line 10, column 1. Ours expresses possession by us; the article examples and agreement prevent treating the French pronoun like English bare ours.

**chinese:** 我们的（人或物）；第一人称复数主有代词，须与冠词连用：le nôtre、la nôtre、les nôtres，形式取决于所代替的人或物的性、数。

Fixed basis: [le nôtre, revision 38903931](https://fr.wiktionary.org/w/index.php?title=le%20n%C3%B4tre&oldid=38903931), line 10, column 1. 我们的（人或物） expresses possession by us; 须与冠词连用 and 性、数 preserve the article construction and agreement.

Excluded uses: Possessive determiner notre without a circumflex; Obsolete standalone possessive adjective; Partisan adjective use
