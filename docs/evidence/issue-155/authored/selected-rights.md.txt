# Selected-text rights proposals for #155

All 200 proposed fields await separate independent quality and rights review.
[rights.json](rights.json) records each exact field, fixed revision, source span,
attribution, modification and pending decision. [Source context](source-context.json)
retains the language/POS frames, construction evidence, contributor registry,
import markers and original-work layers. Author checks do not admit content.

## Contribution and adaptation layers

Fixed Wiktionnaire lexical wording and phonetic/grammatical contributions are
proposed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](COPYING). Lexique's scoped French adaptations and
new English/Chinese explanations retain that licence. They are explanations
of the same selected French use, rather than upstream bilingual quotations.
Required obligations are attribution, fixed source and complete history links,
licence identification and legal text, modification notices, ShareAlike and no
additional restrictions. Project source code retains MIT.

The retained CC legal text and Wikimedia Terms section 7 distinguish imported
material from contributor text. Policy and import-template captures are listed
in [rights.json](rights.json) and preserved in the author archive identified by
[source-captures.json](source-captures.json). They are reused fixed authority
captures, with original paths recorded; they are not new review decisions.

## Historical original works

Thirty captured articles declare DAF8 imports. Original credit is retained to
Académie française, *Dictionnaire de l’Académie française*, eighth edition,
1932–1935. The established [original-work evidence](../issue-129/selected-rights.md)
distinguishes this institutional collective work from later electronic editing.
[CPI L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941/2026-02-20)
gives seventy years from the January following collective publication: using
1935 yields expiry on 2006-01-01. Original credit and work respect remain under
[L121-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278891).
This supports the author rights route for the original edition; separate review
must still assess these selected spans and contributor/adaptation layers.

The linked lexical support for `cachette`, `cache` line 12, defines a place for
hiding something or oneself. Its article references Littré at line 211.
Conservative original credit is retained to Émile Littré (1801–1881),
*Dictionnaire de la langue française*, 1872–1877. The established
[Littré original-work record](../issue-120/source-context.json) and
[independent historical rights discussion](../issue-129/selected-rights.md)
identify this author/edition using the
[BnF record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb30824717s).
[L123-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278937)
gives lifetime plus seventy years, conservatively expired on 1952-01-01.
No modern XMLittré or ARTFL transcription is copied. The historical original-work
decision is recorded as reused evidence; review of this support remains pending.

## Selected boundaries

Article markers and bibliographies do not identify the author of each sentence.
No broad Wiktionnaire licence grants rights to unrelated imported expression.
The proposals select lexical definitions, French/POS/grammar frames and one
applicable isolated IPA variant. Short construction fragments supporting
`hériter` and `obligé` are identified by exact columns. Literary/news quotations,
media, etymologies and other-language definitions supply no learner field.

`talon` selects the anatomical heel at line 11. The CFC cartographic definition
at line 62 supplies no field or supporting span; its marker and exclusion are
retained in [rights.json](rights.json). No CFC content is proposed for admission.
`nan/NOM` binds to the French bread noun `naan` at line 11, its IPA at line 10,
and explicit French variant `nan` at line 23. The captured `nan` interjection
does not supply a noun meaning or pronunciation. The relationship authorizes
source binding only; no new runtime alias is admitted.

The author package includes [notices](NOTICES.md), complete legal text, exact
proposals and source excerpts. Independent acceptance, combined canonical
materials, reproducible conversion and matching native lookup/save/reopen
remain distinct coordinator/reviewer gates.
