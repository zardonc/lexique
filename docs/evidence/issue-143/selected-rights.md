## Original works and territorial boundary

The established collective-work basis applies French CPI L123-3 to the
Académie eighth edition (1932–1935), giving conservative French economic
expiry **2006-01-01**. It establishes neither automatic worldwide original
rights nor authorship of every selected wiki sentence. The accepted factual
relations and short conventional formulations are assessed individually.

Actual [CNRTL Académie 8 renderings](https://www.cnrtl.fr/definition/academie8/distraire)
were captured for eleven longer candidate boundaries. Close selected wording
is present for `distraire`, `impressionnant`, `grève`, `lecture`, `congrès` and
`galerie`. Eleven fourth-edition (1762) comparison responses were also retained;
they do not establish the six exact later formulations as earlier expression.
The `jusque` requests returned no Académie definition and supply no original
clearance. The retained HTML may contain other dictionary payloads; none is
selected as Lexique content. These comparisons are original-boundary evidence,
not replacements for the fixed Wiktionnaire field sources.

[17 USC 102(b)](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html) distinguishes
ideas, processes and concepts from expression. Factual pronunciation, numerical
units and grammatical relations are assessed separately. [104A](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html)
provides restoration conditions for foreign original works; [304](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap3.html)
can preserve a remaining US term. Publication/restoration history, permission
or specific original-expression clearance for the six extended sentences is
not established. This leaves their retained textual source material pending;
it does not determine that every phrase is protected or that infringement occurs.

## Contribution, adaptation and support

The Wiktionnaire contribution and Lexique adaptation/explanation layers retain
**CC BY-SA 4.0**. All fixed versions, full history links, named/anonymous article
credits, original edition attribution where declared, modifications and the
[complete legal text](COPYING) remain with [notices](NOTICES.md). Source code
retains MIT. EN/ZH are new Lexique explanations of the selected meaning, not
upstream bilingual quotations or grants of underlying original-expression rights.

CC sections 2(a), 3(a), 3(b) and 4 cover sharing/adaptation/database use,
attribution, source/licence/warranty identification, modifications, ShareAlike
and no additional downstream restrictions. Wikimedia Terms section 7 requires
source import conditions and US public-domain verification to remain distinct.
The retained actual legal text and policy captures were read. US Copyright
Office authority captures are reused from the reviewer-140 archive; its field
verdicts are not reused.

`uns` support records plural/contrastive grammar, obligatory articles and the
separate partitive `quelques-uns` construction. `chevalier` support identifies
knighthood and the explicitly contemporary member-of-an-order subsense.
`jusque` support records grammar and the limit construction. `pars` support
records the exact noun plural and regular inflection through the golf `par` noun.
These factual support decisions are independent of unselected article prose.

No literary/news quotation, media, audio, etymological exposition or other-
language definition supplies a selected field. External dictionary references
and bibliography identify sources without granting rights to their prose.
Complete raw articles and comparison captures remain outside the repository.
