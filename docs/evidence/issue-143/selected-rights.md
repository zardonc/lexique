## Actual selected boundaries

The proposals use lexical definitions, French/POS/inflection frames and applicable
IPA only. `chevalier` selects the explicitly contemporary order-member subsense,
not the ancient or medieval referent. `mexicain/NOM` selects the lowercase language
noun, not capitalized inhabitants. `uns/PRO:ind` binds to the explicit `les uns`
plural table and the contrastive group sense of `l’un`; the separate partitive
`l’un de` sense explicitly requires `quelques-uns` in the plural and is excluded.
The /œ̃/ IPA is the recorded plural pronoun segment of `le.z‿œ̃`, with article and
liaison excluded. `jusque` retains its construction restriction and does not add
an alias for `jusqu’à`. `pars/NOM` stays unresolved; the singular golf approval
for frozen row 128 grants no exception to row 3128.

Reference lists, external dictionary titles and article-level labels do not
license unselected prose. Literary/news quotations, media, audio, other-language
text and etymology supply no learner field. Raw articles remain outside the
repository as research captures; tracked selected material contains no example
quotation bodies.
