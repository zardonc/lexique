# Issue #114 selected-text rights

**All 200 selected fields pass independent selected-text rights review.** The 50 fixed sources, selected locations and individual reasons are recorded in [rights.json](rights.json), [field review](field-review.json) and [source context](source-context.json). Eligibility and obligations are reviewed separately from actual assembled-package compliance.

## Applicable layers

French definitions are scoped adaptations of selected French lexical definitions. EN/ZH are Lexique explanatory translations of the same meanings. IPA selects recorded French citation variants. Wiktionnaire contributors retain fixed article/history attribution; Lexique contributors are separately credited for selection, adaptations and translations. Those contribution/adaptation layers use **CC BY-SA 4.0**, allowing commercial conversion and redistribution with attribution and ShareAlike.

The **26 DAF8-marked articles** retain original credit to **Académie française, Dictionnaire de l’Académie française, eighth edition, 1932–1935**. The established [#99 original-work basis](../issue-99/source-context.json) applies to the collective edition: [CPI L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941/2026-02-20) sets seventy years from the January following publication; final 1935 publication gives conservative expiry on **2006-01-01**. Wiki transcription/adaptation and new Lexique explanations remain separately licensed. The import marker does not identify who wrote a particular selected sentence; original credit is retained conservatively wherever marked.

No additional external-text import notice attaches to selected lexical wording. Named TLFi/Littré references identify bibliography, not copied selected-definition authorship. Literary/news quotations, media, etymologies, other-language sections and bilingual tables supply no selected wording. `ramasser` adapts only lexical text before the accompanying image syntax.

## Authorities and obligations

Retained first-party authorities are listed in [rights.json](rights.json): CC BY-SA 4.0 legal text; Wikimedia Terms of Use section 7; French copyright policy revision **38903168**; DAF8 import template revision **22113837**. The template says all or part was extracted and may have changed. The policy separates external material and quotation rights from the text licence. All eight contributor parts match complete current registries; fixed article/full history links supply attribution.

The parent-produced resource must:

- Retain supplied creator, copyright, licence and warranty/disclaimer notices, fixed source links, complete history attribution and applicable original-edition notices.
- Identify selection, markup removal, scoped adaptation and explanatory translation; retain supplied prior modification notices.
- Include the [CC BY-SA 4.0 legal text](../../../resources/core-pilot-79/COPYING) and licence link, applying the same licence to adaptations and any applicable database layer under sections 3–4.
- Preserve commercial reuse, extraction and redistribution rights without additional legal restrictions or obstructive technical measures.
- Carry selected-source material and notices required by the parent conversion contract, excluding unselected quotations and media from dictionary field redistribution.

## Limitations

This review read retained fixed authorities and the established original-edition basis; it did not independently refetch live articles or refresh the statute. Canonical conversion, actual combined notices and native/release admission remain parent-owned pending gates.
