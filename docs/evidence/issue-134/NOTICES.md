## Modifications

One selected sense is proposed per exact group. French wording adapts only the
selected semantics, with grammatical and register restrictions retained.
English and Chinese are new explanations of those French meanings, not upstream
bilingual quotations. One applicable isolated IPA variant is selected and
surrounded with slashes. Audio, images and literary/news quotations supply no
learner field.

The masculine plural interrogative and relative `lesquels` each bind to their
matching `lequel` section, and `bijoux` binds to nominal `bijou`. The exact plural
articles supply inflection and IPA. These are source relationships; no lookup
alias is admitted. The selected `voile` is feminine (a boat’s sail), `orange` is
feminine (fruit), and `piper` is the dice-manipulation verb pronounced /pi.pe/.

`nègre` retains the selected noun’s insulting, pejorative and dated/vulgar labels
in all meaning languages. Its contemporary bibliographic context is retained
for review; no quotation from that discussion is redistributed as a field.
`arrestation` uses the explicitly recorded /a.ʁɛs.ta.sjɔ̃/ in the pronunciation
section, while the inconsistent lexical header remains only in source evidence.
`tester` uses the modern object-taking test definition, with the inconsistent
header tag retained in the construction evidence.
