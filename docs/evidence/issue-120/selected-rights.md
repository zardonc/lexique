# Selected rights proposal for #120

The 200 proposed fields use fixed Wiktionnaire text under **CC BY-SA 4.0**.
French fields are scoped adaptations; English and Chinese meanings are new
explanations of those same selected uses. IPA retains one recorded isolated
headword variant with added slashes. [Rights records](rights.json) bind each
field to its actual revision/span, attribution, original-work layers,
modifications and selected boundary. No field has independent rights acceptance.

The fixed [French copyright policy](https://fr.wiktionary.org/w/index.php?title=Convention%3ADroit%20d%E2%80%99auteur&oldid=38903168),
retained Wikimedia Terms of Use section 7, and [complete licence](COPYING)
provide the contribution/adaptation route. Article URLs and complete linked
histories supply attribution. The retained contributor API captures are current
registries, including later editors; they are not a claim that every listed
editor authored the selected sentence. Anonymous contributors are credited
through the full history, with returned counts recorded in source context.

## Original-work layers

- **DAF8:** Académie française, eighth edition, 1932–1935. The established
  [original collective-work basis](../issue-99/source-context.json) applies
  L123-3, seventy years from the following 1 January; using the final 1935
  publication gives 2006-01-01. The separately retained wiki and Lexique
  contributions remain CC BY-SA 4.0. Import markers do not assign authorship
  to individual selected sentences.
- **Littré (`hum`):** the fixed import template identifies Émile Littré’s
  *Dictionnaire de la langue française*, 1872–1877. The retained
  [BnF catalogue](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb30824717s) identifies the
  original author as 1801–1881 and the 1873–1874 edition. The established
  [original-work research](../../d2-resource-admission-research.md) records
  [L123-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278937),
  life plus seventy years; the conservative ordinary expiry is 1952-01-01.
  Modern XMLittré/ARTFL transcription is not used. The wiki and new adaptation
  layers retain CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **CFC (`zéro`):** the fixed import template credits the 1990 second-edition
  *Glossaire de cartographie*, Bulletin nos 123–124, donated under CC BY-SA 4.0.
  The selected number-zero noun definition is line 121 with its noun/IPA frame;
  the cartographic-datum definition at line 163 is unselected. The page-wide
  notice is retained without treating it as selected-sentence attribution.

Other bibliographic references, including TLFi, provide context only. The
selected fields do not copy linked external prose, neighbouring literary/news
quotations, etymologies, media or other-language sections. The image reference
beside `lunette` remains in raw evidence; no image or caption is redistributed
as dictionary content.
