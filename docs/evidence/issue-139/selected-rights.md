## Contribution and adaptation layers

Fixed Wiktionnaire lexical, grammatical and isolated-IPA contributions are used
under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en).
French definitions adapt the selected semantics. English and Chinese are new
Lexique explanations with the same selected sense/POS/use; they are not quotations
from English or Chinese dictionaries. Those adaptations and explanations retain
CC BY-SA 4.0. Source correctness does not establish the new bilingual wording’s
quality; [field review](field-review.json) records the separate actual decisions
for every French, IPA, English and Chinese field and each selected use.

The [complete legal text](COPYING), sections 2(a), 3(a), 3(b) and 4, requires
creator/source attribution, licence identification, supplied copyright and
warranty notices, modification notices, ShareAlike and no additional terms or
technological restrictions. Fixed article and full history links, named
contributor registries and returned anonymous counts are retained. Current
registries can include later contributors and do not prove sentence authorship.

Wikimedia Terms section 7 and copyright-policy revision 38903168 distinguish
external/imported material from the contribution layer. DAF8-template revision
22113837 states that all or part may be extracted and later modified; its presence
alone does not identify a selected sentence’s author. Complete retained authority
captures and URLs are identified in [rights.json](rights.json).

## Original-work boundary

Thirty-one articles declare Académie française eighth-edition imports. Retain
original credit to **Académie française, Dictionnaire de l’Académie française,
eighth edition, 1932–1935**, separately from the current wiki and Lexique layers.
The established independently reviewed [#129 original-work basis](../issue-129/selected-rights.md)
applies only to that original institutional collective work. CPI
[L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941/2026-02-20)
uses seventy years from the January following publication: final 1935 publication
gives 1936-01-01 + 70 years = **2006-01-01**. Author/work respect remains under
[L121-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278891).
This basis does not license a later publisher’s editorial or electronic work,
and the historical review does not review any new #139 selected wording.

TLFi, Littré, Rivarol, Robert, Larousse, Usito and other bibliographic markers
retain reference identities. Bibliography alone does not establish imported
definition text or grant redistribution rights to those external works.
No modern external dictionary transcription is proposed. Literary/news example
references remain research context; their prose is not translated into fields.

`portefeuille` has a line-level CFC import at nautical-chart definition **62**.
That entire subsense is excluded from the pocket wallet selected at **49**;
no CFC content or rights are admitted. The wallet image/caption and `injuste`
grammar-line image/caption are excluded from selected spans. Full source
captures remain available for review, with media outside the content package.
