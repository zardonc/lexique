## Applicable layers

Fixed Wiki lexical/form contributions and transcriptions use **CC BY-SA 4.0**.
Scoped French adaptations, new EN/ZH explanations and IPA variant selection
retain that licence and modification notices. The complete
[legal text](../../../resources/core-pilot-79/COPYING), sections 2–4, permits
commercial adaptation/redistribution with creator/designated credit, source
links, applicable copyright/licence/disclaimer notices, modification indications
and ShareAlike. Adapted text and applicable database rights retain their terms
without additional restrictions or effective technological measures preventing
licensed reuse.

Retained Wikimedia Terms section 7 permits article/history links as attribution,
allows the CC BY-SA route and requires additional imported-source credits to
remain. Fixed histories and complete paginated registries identify contributors;
last editors and registry membership do not establish sentence authorship.

Fixed French copyright policy distinguishes compatible contributions and
original imports. `Import:DAF8` indicates historical imported material and possible
later modification. An `R:DAF8` reference alone remains bibliography. The 43
recorded original-edition layers retain Académie française credit and the
established [collective-work basis](../issue-99/source-context.json): final
publication in 1935, the cited L123-3 seventy-year period and conservative
expiry on 2006-01-01. That established edition basis was assessed for these
selected spans; the live statute was not refreshed.

For `tien/NOM`, fixed eighth-edition article `A8T0594`, tome 2 page 659, supplies
historical noun/contribution and concession wording. The original edition and
digital presenter receive separate credit. New adaptations use CC BY-SA 4.0;
current website layout and ninth-edition prose do not supply fields. Modern
`y mettre du tien` is factual construction corroboration, with no general
ninth-edition redistribution claim. Exact Wiki `tien` supplies only component IPA.
