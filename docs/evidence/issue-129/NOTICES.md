## Modifications

One everyday selected sense is proposed per exact word/POS group. French
definitions retain the selected semantics, construction and register with
markup removed. English and Chinese are new scoped explanatory translations
of that selected French use. One recorded isolated IPA variant is selected
and surrounded by slashes; no liaison, audio or image is copied.

`clef` binds to the separately evidenced `clé` spelling target for a metal
lock-key definition. `clef` itself supplies pronunciation. The frozen identity
remains `clef/NOM`; no runtime alias is admitted. Inflected counterparts remain
grammatical evidence only. The exact lowercase `indien/NOM` drink remains
distinct from the adjective and capitalized demonyms.
