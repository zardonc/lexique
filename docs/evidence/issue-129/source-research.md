# Selected uses for #129

One selected sense per exact frozen group supplies 200 independently reviewed fields.
[Groups](groups.json) and [field review](field-review.json) retain the full
FR/IPA/EN/ZH wording, exclusions and fixed line bindings. English and Chinese
are new scoped explanations of the selected French use, licensed as adaptations
under CC BY-SA 4.0, rather than imports from unverified translation tables.

The French language and exact lexical POS headings accompany each definition.
Pronunciation comes from the same French POS frame, including nominal `manque`
at its `fr-rég` line. No verb-form IPA is substituted for a noun or interjection.
`indien`, `communiste` and `chrétien` retain separate noun and adjective senses.

## Source boundaries

The selected dictionary meanings use French lexical definitions and recorded
phonetic/grammatical contributions. Neighbouring literary/news/social-media
examples, lyrics, media, external linked dictionary prose and other-language
content supply no imported learner fields. Short construction spans have exact
columns; their surrounding raw source stays in the ignored evidence archive.

`indien`'s cited song lyric is context only; no lyric is copied into the drink
definition. Orangina names an ingredient without importing logos or advertising.
The `clef` photographs and `ha` illustration are likewise excluded.

Thirty source articles retain DAF8 import markers. `passager` additionally
declares an import from Littré. The [selected rights](selected-rights.md) and
per-field [rights records](rights.json) distinguish those original works from
the Wiktionnaire and Lexique contribution/adaptation layers. Source reference
lists or import markers do not identify a selected sentence's individual author.

All 200 fields pass separate independent semantic and selected-span rights
review; all 50 uses and source spelling/construction frames pass. Exact decisions
and original proposals are retained in field-review.json and authored snapshots.
The Chinese boucler field specifies a buckle (搭扣). Canonical assembly remains
parent-owned and pending.
