## Contribution and adaptation layers

CC BY-SA 4.0. Wikimedia Terms of Use section 7 provides article/history-link
attribution and requires imported content's separate terms to remain applicable.

reuse existing successful policy/API/HTML captures with their actual timestamps,
fixed policy revisions and original capture location retained.

CC BY-SA 4.0 sections 2(a)(1), 2(a)(4), 3(a), 3(b) and 4 permit sharing and
adaptation, including commercial use, with attribution, licence identification,
modification notices, ShareAlike and no additional restrictions. Its public-domain
boundary does not relicense the separate original works. The [complete legal
text](COPYING), [notices](NOTICES.md), article/history links and field-level
modification records accompany the candidate package.

French fields are scoped adaptations of the selected French lexical semantics.
English and Chinese are new explanatory translations by Lexique contributors
under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 adaptation route. IPA selects a recorded variant and
adds slashes; it does not import recordings or images. All spelling relations

this proposal.

## Imported original works

**Académie française:** thirty captured articles declare DAF8 imports.
The original collective eighth-edition dictionary was published in 1932–1935.
The established [original-work evidence](../issue-99/source-context.json) applies
Code de la propriété intellectuelle article L123-3: seventy years from 1 January
following publication of a collective work. Using the final 1935 publication
conservatively gives 1966-01-01. This proposal credits Académie française where
the original work contributes; current Wiktionnaire transcription/adaptation and
the new Lexique adaptation retain their own CC BY-SA 4.0 layer.

**Émile Littré:** `passager` declares both Littré and DAF8 imports. The package
reuses the separately established [Littré original-work evidence](../issue-120/source-context.json)
for Émile Littré (1801–1881), *Dictionnaire de la langue française*, 1872–1877.
The recorded L123-1 lifetime-plus-seventy-year basis conservatively expires on
1952-01-01, with the BnF author/edition authority retained. No modern XMLittré,
ARTFL or other external transcription is copied. The selected nominal transport
sense at line 92 and nominal IPA at line 87 remain bound to the actual fixed
Wiktionnaire revision, with both declared original layers credited.

An article-level import marker says all or part came from an original work and
may have changed; it does not locate that original wording at a particular
selected sentence. The proposal supplies the permitted original-work routes
alongside the fixed contributor/adaptation route; the independent reviewer
still assesses each actual selected/imported boundary.

## Redistribution scope

Selected learner fields contain lexical definitions, isolated IPA and scoped
translations only. Literary/news/social-media quotations, lyrics, media,
etymologies and referenced external dictionary prose are excluded. Construction
support selects short French grammatical fragments at exact columns rather
than the surrounding example sentences.

The parent-produced resource must retain actual fixed-source/version material,
full contributor/history credits, separate original-work attributions, complete
applicable legal text and modification notices, and distribute adaptation text
under CC BY-SA 4.0 without additional restrictions. The project source-code MIT
licence does not replace these content terms. Contributor pagination proves
the retained attribution registry, not independent quality or selected-sentence
authorship. Final combined notices and source obligations remain pending.
