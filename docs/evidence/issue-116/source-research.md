## Construction, spelling and flexion

`tien/PRO:pos` is the masculine singular component of the modern pronoun
`le tien`; its complete source table records le tien/les tiens/la tienne/les
tiennes. The bare obsolete adjective supplies only the component pronunciation,
not the selected semantics. Frozen `oeuf` retains its attested typographic
relation to `œuf`, the food sense and singular /œf/. Both source tables give
the plural spelling and /ø/ pronunciation, with /œf/ also recorded.

`sauvage/NOM` selects an unsociable person, with its potentially disparaging
tone retained, separately from obsolete ethnic labels. `franc/ADJ` has feminine
franche, while the unselected ethnic adjective has franque; `franc/NOM` selects
a currency, with the captured CHF derivative supporting its current Swiss use.
`espagnol/NOM` is the lowercase generally uncountable language noun; `page/NOM`
selects the feminine page rather than the masculine servant. `star` is a French
feminine noun even for a male celebrity. `disputer` selects pronominal
`se disputer`; `miss` retains traditional plural misses and rectified miss.
`commun/NOM` selects singular collective `le commun des` + people, separately
from the adjective and plural outbuildings.
