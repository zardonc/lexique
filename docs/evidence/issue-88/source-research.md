## Grammatical and spelling bindings

- `tous/PRO:ind`: revision 39770627 lines21–22 records masculine plural
  pronoun pronunciation /tus/. Related `tout` revision 39886654 line446
  defines the previously mentioned set; line26 distinguishes pronoun /tus/
  from determiner /tu/. The separately labelled literary use is excluded.
- `ok`: French `OK` revision 39863637 line33 explicitly lists lowercase
  `ok` as a variant. Lines13–14 support the adjective; lines20–21 support
  an agreement interjection. Both proposed spelling records await review.
- `quelques/ADJ:ind`: fixed plural indefinite-adjective/determiner entry
  lines2–5 supports the small-number use before a plural noun. The distinct
  numeral/pronoun construction is excluded.
- `lire/NOM`: revision 39881049 line342 selects Turkey’s currency. The
  former Italian currency at line340 and the verb remain separate.
- `plein/PRE`: revision 39881441 lines383–387 records the preposition and
  contributor constructions `avoir X plein Y`, including money in pockets.
  The adjective and adverb/quantity constructions are excluded.
- `madame/NOM`: revision 39819067 lines9–11 records the polite title and
  plural `mesdames`; the regional anonymous-woman sense is excluded.
