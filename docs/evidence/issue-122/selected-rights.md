Each of the **200 fields** has its exact source revision and original span in
[rights.json](rights.json), its attribution and current contributor registry in
[source-context.json](source-context.json), and its modification record in
[groups.json](groups.json). Original author proposals are retained beside the independently accepted
selected-span decisions; all 200 fields pass. See [rights-review.md](rights-review.md).

The selected French lexical definitions and phonetic/grammatical records come
from fixed Wiktionnaire revisions under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Lexique's scoped French
adaptations and newly authored English/Chinese explanatory translations retain
that licence. The translations explain the selected use, including its register,
gender, construction or spelling restriction; they are not quoted bilingual
dictionary entries. [COPYING](COPYING) and [NOTICES.md](NOTICES.md) supply the
legal text, source/history attribution and modification notices.

The retained legal authorities cover CC BY-SA 4.0 sections 2(a), 3(a), 3(b) and 4,
Wikimedia Terms of Use section 7, Wiktionnaire copyright policy revision
38903168, DAF8 import-template revision 22113837 and the separately captured
Littré import template. Sharing and adaptation, including commercial
redistribution, require attribution, licence and modification identification,
ShareAlike and no additional restrictions. Media and imported originals retain
their own rights. Exact authority capture paths and fixed template revisions are
recorded in [rights.json](rights.json).
