# Issue #122 fixed sources and selected uses

All **50 exact groups / 50 senses / 200 four-field candidates** have fixed source
bindings. No group is reused or excepted. The fixed denominator remains 4,087.
[Groups](groups.json), [field proposals](field-review.json) and
[selected spans](source-excerpts.json) bind each field to its own use.

The 52 selected French articles were captured through the first-party revision
API and reproduced through six exact revision-ID requests. All named contributor
pages terminate: six main responses for 48 titles and two support responses for
six titles. Two support captures (`venir`, `recevoir`) supply no field.
[Capture records](source-captures.json) retain requests, responses, wikitext and
the complete preparation/reproduction path.

`bel` and `nouvel` retain masculine singular pre-noun vowel/silent-h restrictions
with their own exact inflection articles and separately captured lemma meanings.
`allo` is independently evidenced as an unaccented spelling of telephone `allô`;
`revolver` is the traditional spelling of `révolver`. These are source bindings,
with `alias_admission=not_admitted`; no runtime alias is proposed.

The adjective and interjection `mince` are separate groups. The interjection's
IPA comes from the article-wide pronunciation section. `venu/ADJ` uses the
adjectival agreement table and the qualified `bien/mal venu` development or
production meaning, preserving both qualifiers in all semantic fields.
`reçu/ADJ` describes an accepted/established use or view; `reçu/NOM` is the written
money receipt. Neither is a substituted past participle.

`assister` selects transitive help, and `échouer` selects intransitive failure.
`emprunter`, `noter`, `verser`, `dépenser`, `assassiner`, `avertir`, `affronter`,
`adresser`, `vider` and `parer` retain their selected object/construction scope.
`race` selects animal breeding, not a human biological classification.
`gorge` selects the internal throat; `matériel` selects production materials and
tools; `produit` includes commercial items, services and software.
`humeur` uses silent h, and `chouette/ADJ` retains informal register.

[Selected rights](selected-rights.md), [rights proposals](rights.json),
[notices](NOTICES.md) and [legal text](COPYING) distinguish contributor,
historical-original and new-translation layers. Independent semantic, spelling,
selected-rights and original-work review remains pending for all 200 fields.
