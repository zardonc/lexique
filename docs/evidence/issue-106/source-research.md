## Source and rights boundary

The fixed Wiktionnaire revisions, exact selected spans and French/POS/headword
frames are in [sources](sources.json), [source context](source-context.json) and
[source excerpts](source-excerpts.json). [Capture records](source-captures.json)
point to the original API and raw revision files. Current contributor names and
complete linked history provide attribution; they do not resolve the author of
an individual selected sentence. Current returned revisions are used throughout.

Wiktionnaire contributors' transcription/adaptation layer is credited under
CC BY-SA 4.0. Lexique's FR adaptations and new EN/ZH explanatory translations
carry their own modification notice under the same licence. IPA extracts one
recorded variant, with slash notation, without audio or media.

DAF8 references/imports retain Académie française's original-edition attribution
separately. The accepted [issue #99 original-rights record](../issue-99/sources.json)
supplies the edition-level collective-work basis: publication in 1932–1935,
conservative public-domain expiry on 2006-01-01 under
[CPI L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941/2026-02-20).
This does not establish a particular import's wording correspondence. Other
page bibliography and example-source markers remain attributed metadata; no
permission for their unselected quoted prose is claimed. Selected-sentence
rights eligibility remains for the independent reviewer.

Redistribution requires fixed article/history attribution, the actual CC BY-SA
4.0 legal text and link, modification notices, share-alike for adaptations and
applicable original-author/import notices. Project-owned code retains MIT.
Final combined packaging obligations are an integration gate after admission.

The suggested `dictionary-modern-batch50-next19-20261119` scope/map was inspected;
its frozen selection has no overlap with this ticket. The archived #99 helpers
supplied the established acquisition and author-handoff pattern. No prior
admitted group is reused or changed by this batch.

## Independent reviewer focus

`soit/CON` has its own conjunction section at 53, frame at 55 and alternative
definition at 56 in revision 39992483. Preserve `soit… soit` or `soit… ou`, two
or more choices, and `/swa/`; the agreement adverb at 110–111 has `/swat/`.
`enfuir/VER` retains the exact lemma, with only the `s’enfuir` construction for
the chosen escape sense. No bare standalone lexical use or new alias is asserted.

The six adjective/noun pairs (`imbécile`, `malin`, `innocent`, `second`, `vert`,
`saint`) have separate section and pronunciation frames, selected-sense IDs and
definitions. `imbécile/ADJ` selects foolish behaviour or an action; the person
noun is explicitly derogatory. The obsolete disability/psychiatric senses are
excluded. `cool/ADJ` selects only the relaxed-person use in the French section.

`espoir/NOM` preserves the selected hoping sense's rare plural, sometimes poetic
or formal. `toilette/NOM` is personal washing, without importing a plural-room
meaning. `information/NOM` selects an item of knowledge on a medium, away from
the CFC-marked cartography definition. `vert/ADJ` keeps the everyday chromatic
comparison; wavelength detail is not part of this scoped explanation.

All 196 proposed fields await independent same-use quality and selected-text
rights review. The four `d/PRE` fields have a separate source blocker. There is
no newly approved annotation or scope exception. Real resource conversion,
lookup/save/reopen, packaging, learner acceptance and wider release gates keep
their separate completion states.
