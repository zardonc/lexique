## Selected contribution and adaptation layer

The selected French lexical definitions, POS/construction frames and isolated
IPA are from fixed Wiktionnaire contributions. French definitions adapt those
selected semantics; English and Chinese are newly authored Lexique explanations
of the same use. The proposed contribution/adaptation licence is
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en), with
[complete legal text](COPYING). Sections 2(a), 3(a), 3(b) and 4 permit sharing,
adaptation and applicable database extraction with attribution, source/licence
identification, modification notices, ShareAlike and no additional restrictions.
Project-owned source code remains MIT; the content does not become MIT.

The retained Wikimedia Terms of Use, section 7, permit article/history attribution
and preserve imported material’s separate obligations. Copyright-policy revision
38903168 and DAF8-template revision 22113837 distinguish those boundaries.
[Capture and reuse records](rights.json) identify the actual retained legal and
policy materials. Complete paginated named registries, returned anonymous counts
and full article histories accompany [source context](source-context.json).
Current registries extend beyond fixed revisions and do not prove sentence authorship.

## Original institutional dictionary

Thirty articles declare imports from **Académie française, Dictionnaire de
l’Académie française, eighth edition, 1932–1935**. The established
[#99 original-work record](../issue-99/source-context.json) and historically
independent [#129 original-work review](../issue-129/selected-rights.md) support
the original institutional collective edition, separately from later electronic
editorial work. [CPI L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941/2026-02-20)
gives seventy years from January following publication: final publication in
1935 yields **1936-01-01 + 70 years = 2006-01-01**. Original author/work respect
remains under [L121-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278891).

That historical original-work review is retained only for the same original
edition and legal basis. It accepts none of this batch’s new selections,
translations, transcription layers or import boundaries. Those still require
independent review. Import markers alone do not establish which original author
wrote a selected sentence; original credit is retained conservatively.
No modern Académie electronic edition, TLFi or Littré text is admitted by a
reference marker. Subsequent Wiktionnaire and Lexique adaptation layers remain
under the proposed CC BY-SA 4.0 route.

## Excluded CFC boundary

`couche` contains `Import:CFC` on the cartographic coating definition at line 86.
That definition supplies none of the diaper fields selected at line 29. Only the
marker, exact location and exclusion are retained in tracked context; the full
article stays research evidence. The separately captured CFC import template,
revision 29619964, identifies the notice. No blanket CFC licence assumption,
copied cartographic definition or CFC-derived learner field is proposed.

Unselected literary/news examples, lyrics, images, audio, etymologies, external
definitions and other-language text are excluded from the selected learner
fields. Reference lists establish bibliographic identity, not sentence origin.
