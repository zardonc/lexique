## Original institutional dictionary

Thirty fixed articles declare DAF8 imports. Each declared boundary was inspected
separately from its selected lexical/IPA and construction spans. The original
work is Académie française’s collective eighth edition, 1932–1935. Its
[official preface](https://www.academie-francaise.fr/le-dictionnaire-les-neufs-prefaces/preface-de-la-huitieme-edition-1932-1935)
identifies the work of the commission and assembled Académie; the institution’s
[chronology](https://www.academie-francaise.fr/linstitution-lhistoire/les-grandes-dates)
records completion in 1935. Actual captures and the independent assessment are
recorded in [original-work checks](modern-use-evidence.json).

[CPI L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941/2026-10-28)
sets seventy years from January following publication for collective works,
with separately published elements treated individually. Using final publication
in 1935 conservatively gives **1936-01-01 + 70 years = 2006-01-01** for the whole
original edition. Original author/work credit is retained. The established
[#99 original-work record](../issue-99/source-context.json) and
[#129 review](../issue-129/selected-rights.md) remain historical evidence;
the present batch’s new selections have their own decisions.

Article-level imports and bibliography markers do not identify a selected
sentence’s original author. The separately credited historical layer does not
relicense subsequent Wiktionnaire transcription/editorial contributions or
Lexique explanations. Those retain CC BY-SA 4.0. Modern Académie electronic
editions, TLFi and Littré prose are not selected through reference markers.
