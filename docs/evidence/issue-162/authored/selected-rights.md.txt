All 148 proposed fields await independent selected-span and original-expression
review. No field is independently accepted or admitted.

The [rights records](rights.json) bind each FR/IPA/EN/ZH proposal to a fixed
revision, exact raw span, source/contributor attribution and modification.

recordings. The [complete legal text](COPYING) and [source notices](NOTICES.md)

## Original works and selected boundaries

Twenty-eight of the 38 fixed source articles have DAF8 import or reference
markers. Where an original eighth-edition layer applies, credit remains with
Académie française, Dictionnaire de l’Académie française, eighth edition,
1932–1935. The established [#129 original-work basis](../issue-129/selected-rights.md)
uses the institutional collective-work rule in CPI L123-3: final 1935 publication
gives a conservative 2006-01-01 expiry. That prior original-work assessment
does not establish the author or eligibility of a new selected sentence.
The fixed contribution/transcription and new adaptation layers keep their
separate CC BY-SA 4.0 conditions.

Article-level import notices and bibliographic TLFi, Littré or FranceTerme
references do not prove selected-expression origin. Each new expression remains
pending an exact-origin audit. Literary/news examples, etymologies, images,
audio and other-language entries supply no learner field.

## Attribution and redistribution

The eight retained contributor API responses form five complete pagination
chains covering 38 source titles. Named registries and returned anonymous
counts are preserved in [source context](source-context.json); complete article
history links provide attribution for anonymous and other contributors. These
current registries may include later editors and do not prove sentence-level
authorship at the selected revision.

The copied legal authorities retain their original capture dates and source
paths in [rights.json](rights.json). CC BY-SA 4.0 sections 2(a), 3(a), 3(b) and 4
provide sharing/adaptation, attribution, licence/modification notices,
ShareAlike and database conditions; Wikimedia Terms section 7 retains imported
material’s separate obligations and permits article/history attribution.
Complete source/history credits, applicable original-work credit, selected
source material, modification notices and the legal text accompany these
proposals. Actual combined conversion and redistribution remain coordinator
owned and pending.
