## Original expression and support boundaries

DAF8 import or reference markers retain separate credit to **Académie française,
Dictionnaire de l’Académie française, eighth edition, 1932–1935**. The established
[original-work basis](../issue-129/selected-rights.md) treats that institutional
dictionary as a collective work under
[CPI L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941/2026-02-20).
Seventy years from 1 January following final publication in 1935 gives a
conservative expiry of **2006-01-01**. Original author/work credit is retained
separately from the wiki and Lexique adaptation layers. This historical basis
does not independently accept the new selected sentences or their provenance.

`barrage` selects the road-barrier definition at line 9, explicitly referencing
DAF8. The hydraulic definition referencing Rivarol at line 11 is excluded.
`capitale` selects the city noun at line 8; the CFC-imported cartographic-letter
definition at line 42 is excluded from fields and support. General reference
lists, including TLFi and Littré, do not prove selected-sentence origin or grant
rights to a modern external transcription. Every new original-expression
boundary remains subject to separate selected-span review.

`pull`, `baby` and `mark` expand linked lexical meanings through fixed
`pull-over`, `baby-foot` and `deutschemark` definitions. `tranquillement` uses
the fixed calm sense of `tranquille`. Supporting definitions have their own
revision, span and contributor/rights records. Spelling is bound to each exact
frozen French headword; these semantic links add no query aliases.

`fiancer` and `effondrer` combine the selected lexical semantics with the
captured pronominal grammar. Literary/news examples supply no copied or adapted
prose. IPA records the applicable bare infinitive, separately from the selected
pronominal construction. Unselected examples, media, etymologies and
other-language entries remain outside the proposed learner fields.

## Redistribution obligations

Retain the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence/link, article/version/history and contributor
attribution, applicable original-work credits, modification notices and selected
source material. Share the adapted text under CC BY-SA 4.0 without additional
restrictions. Project code remains MIT. The retained Wikimedia Terms and
Wiktionnaire policy/template captures distinguish contributor text from imported
works and media. Their exact authority versions and reuse origins are listed in
[rights.json](rights.json).

Actual combined notices, corresponding source materials and converted artifact
obligations are coordinator-owned and pending. This author package establishes
no canonical admission or release acceptance.
