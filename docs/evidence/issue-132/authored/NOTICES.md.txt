## Modifications and selected content

One sense is proposed for each of 49 ordinary groups. The owner-approved
row-2560 annotation has zero fields and four missing fields; its rejected old-use
proposal is retained as evidence only. French wording is scoped to the selected
use, with markup removed and grammatical/register restrictions retained.
English and Chinese fields are new explanatory translations of that French
use. One recorded isolated IPA variant is selected and enclosed in slashes.
Unselected senses, etymologies, quoted examples, images, audio and other-language
content do not supply the dictionary fields. Spelling and grammar are source
bindings, with no query aliases admitted.

Where imported, the original Académie française eighth edition (1932–1935)
retains its separate public-domain original-work basis and credit (conservative
expiry 2006-01-01 under the seventy-year collective-work term). The referenced
Émile Littré *Dictionnaire de la langue française* (1872–1877) layer is separately
credited where applicable (original author died in 1881; ordinary conservative
expiry 1952-01-01). Wiktionnaire transcription/adaptation and the Lexique
contribution layers retain CC BY-SA 4.0. Reference links to modern dictionaries
do not provide a licence to copy their external prose.

Full [source context](source-context.json) records named contributors and
anonymous counts. The complete current paginated registries credit 3,113
named contributor memberships across 50 articles, plus anonymous identities
through each complete history. Those current registries can include editors
after the fixed revision and do not identify selected-sentence authorship.
