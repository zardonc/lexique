## Binding and scope decisions

Every frozen spelling is present exactly in its French source. The two POS
groups of **promis** and **scientifique** retain separate source section/line
bindings and distinct sense IDs; neither is merged with its other group.
**b** uses the lowercase letter-name frame, and **enculé** uses the explicitly
adjectival `de + nom` construction. These are source bindings, with no query alias.
Gender, intransitive/transitive and register limitations remain in the selected
use and all relevant meanings. **Déménager** selects the intransitive move-home
use; **plonger** selects transitive immersion; **péter** retains its coarse
colloquial intransitive bodily sense. **Mode** selects the feminine fashion noun;
**éternel** selects ordinary hyperbolic duration. Technical alternatives are excluded.

For **promis/NOM**, [Larousse collège](https://www.larousse.fr/dictionnaires/college/promis/32519)
classifies the fiancé noun as “VIEUX ou RÉGIONAL”.
[Usito](https://usito.usherbrooke.ca/lexies/mots/promis) marks it dated.
Both primary-page captures remain fixed under the author capture root and
are recorded as read-only register/POS validation. No proprietary definition
or example supplies a field. The regional candidate retains dated/familiar
restrictions and leaves modern-use eligibility to independent review.

For **chèque**, [Service Public](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2402),
verified 2026-06-16, confirms contemporary account/beneficiary/payment facts.
This is read-only validation. The lexical definition is a scoped adaptation
of fixed Wiktionnaire semantics; no government prose or detailed legal rule is copied.

The raw source context contains **24 DAF8-marked articles**. Original edition
credit and its separate rights basis are retained conservatively; an import
or bibliography marker alone does not establish selected-sentence authorship.
No unselected literary/news quotation, etymology, other-language definition,
image or audio file supplies proposed content. The plonger headword line contains
unselected image markup; the proposed IPA extracts only its recorded phonetic variant.
