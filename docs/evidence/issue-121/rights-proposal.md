# Issue #121 selected-rights author proposal

All 200 fields have fixed source/version/span records, attribution, selected
boundaries, modifications and source-specific rights proposals. Acceptance is
pending the separate reviewer; no independent or final-artifact approval is asserted.

The first-party **Wiktionnaire copyright policy**, fixed revision 38903168,
and **DAF8 import template**, fixed revision 22113837, are preserved together
with the complete Creative Commons legal text and Wikimedia Terms of Use.
They are reused byte-for-byte from the established #119 rights-authority archive;
local copies and original retrieval metadata are recorded in `rights.json`.
CC BY-SA 4.0 §§2(a), 3(a), 3(b) and 4 allow sharing/adaptation, including commercial
use, with attribution, source/licence identification, modification notices,
ShareAlike and no additional restrictions. Wikimedia Terms §7 supports article/history
attribution while preserving imported-content obligations. The selected contribution
and adaptation layer is proposed under that licence, with complete legal text in `COPYING`.

**24 DAF8-marked articles** retain original credit to Académie française,
*Dictionnaire de l’Académie française*, eighth edition, 1932–1935.
The established [#99 original-work basis](../issue-99/source-context.json)
and [CPI L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941/2026-02-20)
record a collective work’s seventy-year term from the January following publication.
Final 1935 publication gives conservative expiry **2006-01-01** for the whole edition.
Original rights, fixed contributor transcription/adaptation and new Lexique
explanations are separate layers. No current dictionary’s site-wide licence is
treated as permission for unrelated imported work. Page import and bibliography
markers do not establish individual selected-sentence authorship.

Each rights field identifies the actual lexical or IPA span. French fields
adapt that lexical meaning; EN/ZH explanations translate its scoped use rather
than importing bilingual text. Recorded phonetic notation is selected without
audio. Language/POS/gender/construction evidence is credited through the same
fixed source histories. Other-language prose, quoted literary/news examples,
etymologies and actual media files are excluded.

**Read-only validation** is separately recorded for promis register/POS and
current chèque payment facts. The only short externally quoted classifier is
Larousse collège’s old-or-regional register. No proprietary definition or
government prose is redistributed as a field. The selected modern regional
eligibility of promis and the nominal/adjectival mappings for b and enculé
remain explicit independent review priorities in `admission.json`.

The [notices](NOTICES.md), per-field [rights](rights.json),
[source context](source-context.json) and [exact excerpts](source-excerpts.json)
form the proposal. Integration must preserve these source materials, contribution
credits, complete licence, modification information and separately credited
original-work layers in the actual combined output. Author checks and reproduction
do not establish independent rights acceptance, native execution or release readiness.
