[CPI L113-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278882)

under its name and contributions combined into the whole. This fits the
original institutional dictionary. The retained official

Conservative expiry is therefore **1936-01-01 + 70 years = 2006-01-01**.
