contributors**, under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Full article histories credit named
and anonymous contributors; complete paginated named registries and anonymous
counts are retained in [source-context.json](source-context.json).

their usage restrictions, select one recorded IPA variant with enclosing slashes,
and provide new English/Chinese explanatory translations. The contribution and
adaptation layer remains CC BY-SA 4.0; project source code remains MIT.

française*, eighth edition (1932–1935), for the 39 source articles that import
or reference that edition. This comprises 24 explicit imports and 15
bibliographic references; credit is conservative at the selected original-work boundary.

2006-01-01 using final 1935 publication plus seventy years from the following
January. Fixed Wiktionnaire transcription/adaptation and Lexique adaptations
retain their own CC BY-SA 4.0 layer. Import markers do not identify each sentence’s author.
