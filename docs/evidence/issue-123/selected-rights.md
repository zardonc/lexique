# Issue #123 selected-rights proposals

The package binds **200 authored fields to 52 fixed French Wiktionnaire
articles**. Every field has its exact revision, line, source excerpt,
modification, attribution and selected-boundary proposal in
[rights.json](rights.json). All independent rights decisions remain pending.

The retained copyright-policy revision **38903168**, DAF8 import-template
revision **22113837**, Wikimedia Terms of Use and CC BY-SA 4.0 legal text are
reused from the established #119 authority captures. The local copies and
their original capture metadata are listed in `rights.json`. Licence sections
2(a), 3(a), 3(b) and 4 permit sharing and adaptation, including commercial use,
with attribution, source/licence identification, modification notices,
ShareAlike and no additional restrictions. Wikimedia Terms section 7 supplies
article/history attribution and preserves obligations for imported material.

**45 source articles import or reference DAF8.** Conservative original-work
credit is retained for **Académie française, Dictionnaire de l’Académie
française, eighth edition, 1932–1935**. The admitted
[#99 original-edition record](../issue-99/source-context.json) supplies the
collective-work basis under
[CPI L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941/2026-02-20).
Seventy years from the January following final 1935 publication gives
conservative expiry **2006-01-01**. The original edition layer is separate from
fixed contributor transcription/adaptation and Lexique’s new scoped
adaptations/translations, which retain CC BY-SA 4.0. An import or bibliography
marker does not identify the author of a selected sentence. Live statute
retrieval is not repeated; the established admitted authority and edition
basis are retained for the independent reviewer.

The selected French definitions are scoped adaptations of lexical meanings.
English and Chinese fields are new explanatory translations of the same
selected French use, with register and construction restrictions retained;
they are not copied from upstream bilingual tables. IPA selects one recorded
isolated-headword variant and adds slashes; no audio or other media supplies a
field.

The CFC import in **feuille**, raw line **39**, belongs to an unselected
cartographic definition. The selected writable-sheet use at line **28** and
its IPA at line **8** do not use that text or any associated construction.
Other external reference lists, including TLFi/Littré/Larousse/Robert, are
preserved as bibliography without admitting external entries. The TLFi marker
on **davantage** accompanies the basic lexical/usage statement; proposed
texts select the complementless adverb in short original explanations.
For **auprès**, only its recorded IPA and modern construction note support
the component binding; its Rivarol reference does not supply definition prose.
The separately captured **auprès de** entry supplies the prepositional
meaning. The short **un grand v / un petit v** constructions evidence the
masculine lowercase letter noun; no attributed literary example supplies a
field or construction binding.

All **3,546 named article-registry entries**, returned anonymous counts and
full linked histories are retained in [source-context.json](source-context.json).
Both paginated request sets terminate. Current registries can include later
contributors; linked histories preserve attribution at the fixed revision and
for anonymous edits. A contributor registry does not prove sentence-level
authorship.

[NOTICES.md](NOTICES.md) and [COPYING](COPYING) provide proposed attribution,
modification and licence materials. Independent review must decide the actual
selected spans; the integration owner must satisfy obligations in the combined
converted and redistributed artifact. This author package does not assert
independent acceptance, runtime admission or final packaging compliance.
