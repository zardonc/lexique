## Construction and spelling bindings

`auprès/PRE` is the exact component in **auprès de + complement**, supported by the component article’s note and the separately captured French preposition. Its IPA remains /o.pʁɛ/ for auprès; the required de is visible in FR/EN/ZH and the use.

`v/NOM` denotes the exact lowercase letter, with source category **lettre**, masculine invariant headword /ve/ and the short constructions **un grand v / un petit v**. It does not use the homographic verso abbreviation /vɛʁ.so/ or uppercase V.

`suicider/VER` selects **se suicider**. The source explicitly marks this sense pronominal; the required se is visible in all three meaning fields. IPA records the infinitive component.

`labo/NOM` retains its exact abbreviation and recorded /la.bo/. Its synonym and apocope evidence are bound to the separately captured laboratoire research-location sense. `fac/NOM` retains the France/informal register and /fak/, with the university-division sense of separately captured faculté; English faculty does not mean teaching staff here.

The two physique groups use their own adjective and masculine-noun frames. whisky is explicitly limited to the source’s malted-barley spirit; station retains the metro/small-railway-station restriction; davantage selects the complementless adverb. All binding decisions and exact grammar lines are in [field-review.json](field-review.json) and [source-excerpts.json](source-excerpts.json). All alias admissions remain not_admitted.
