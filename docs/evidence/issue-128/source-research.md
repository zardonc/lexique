Every proposed field has a fixed revision, selected line and applicable

## Meaning and construction boundaries

- `pointer/VER` selects directing an instrument or weapon **vers** a point,
  pronounced **/pwɛ̃.te/**. The dog noun’s different IPA is excluded.
- `inscrire/VER` selects recording someone/something **sur** a register/list;
  `lutter/VER` retains the source’s usual **contre** complement.
- `digne/ADJ` selects deserving something, with **de + noun/infinitive**.
  Only the selected unattributed lexicographical example list supplies that
  construction. Absolute dignified/respectable uses are excluded.
- `satisfaire/VER` selects fulfilling an obligation or meeting a requirement.
  The intransitive label and **à** for an indirect complement are explicit in
  the source; all three meaning fields retain **satisfaire à**.
- `sacrer/VER` selects the modern **Québec** swearing use, including typical
  religious swearwords and its possible surprise/joy/pain expression. It can
  be intransitive or use **contre**. The source’s obsolete non-Québec route,
  consecration, throwing/expelling and **se sacrer de** are excluded. All three
  meaning fields retain the Québec restriction.
- `donne/NOM` selects the ordinary card-dealing action, as a **feminine noun**.
  It does not use the `donner` verb-form frame, the resulting hand or figurative
  resources meaning.
- `mandat/NOM` selects the **political term-of-office duration**, supported by
  both the political mission and duration definitions. It is not a warrant or
  payment order.
- `torturer/VER`, `exécuter/VER`, `épuiser/VER` and `décrocher/VER` retain their
  selected direct objects: literal torture of someone; carrying out a thing;
  fully using up a thing; taking an attached thing down. The distinct emotional,
  death-penalty, person-fatigue, phone/aviation or other senses are excluded
  from the corresponding selection.

## POS, spelling and pronunciation

- `géant/ADJ` is very large size; `géant/NOM` is a very tall real person.
  The mythical creature is not selected. Their separate adjective/noun source
  frames and the feminine `géante` are recorded.
- Lowercase `japonais/ADJ` relates to Japan/inhabitants/culture; lowercase
  `japonais/NOM` is the **masculine language noun**, including the source’s SOV
  word-order classification. There is no alias to the capitalized person name
  `Japonais`, or to the feminine adjective.
- `original/ADJ` describes an original versus copied item; `original/NOM`
  denotes the original item itself. Their distinct POS frames retain
  `originale`/`originaux` agreement without an alias.
- `pressé/ADJ` and `élevé/ADJ` use adjective frames, not verbal participles.
  `figure`, `attente`, `chute` and `donne` use noun frames rather than their
  verb homographs. `net` and `vilain` use the selected adjective sections.
- `pêche/NOM` selects the peach fruit with its exact circumflex, feminine
  noun frame and **/pɛʃ/**, excluding fishing, colour, energy and the verb form.
- `vif/ADJ` selects lively/brisk vigour and activity. The **vif/vive** agreement
  frame supports the masculine frozen form. **/vif/** comes from the general
  isolated French pronunciation line; the audio itself is not imported.
- `info/NOM` retains the **familier** information sense, excluding computing.
  `saloperie/NOM` retains the **familier** dirt/filth sense and coarse tone.
- Multiple recorded IPA variants for `jouet`, `moto`, `passeport`, `pressé`
  and `élevé` are not merged into a synthetic reading: one recorded isolated
  variant is selected and its exact substring/column is retained.

All 50 spelling relations are exact French source bindings. They admit no
runtime alias. Unknown CEFR values remain null. Ordinary meanings such as a
lift, glove, fever, toy, ocean or method keep their source definitions and
grammatical frames; source lexicon tags alone are not a specialist exception.
