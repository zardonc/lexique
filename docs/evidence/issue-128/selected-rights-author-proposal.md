spans under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. French wording is adapted to the selected use;
English/Chinese explanations are new Lexique translations of that same use.

retained Wikimedia Terms of Use section 7 and [complete licence](COPYING)
provide the contribution/adaptation route. Preserve article links and complete
histories, licence identification, attribution, modification notices and
ShareAlike; the terms permit commercial sharing/adaptation with these
conditions and prohibit added restrictions.

The complete paginated named registry and anonymous counts are in
[source context](source-context.json). The registry is current and can include
editors after a fixed revision; full histories credit anonymous contributors.
Neither registry nor import notices determine a selected sentence’s author.
The five reused authority records retain unchanged first-party response bytes,
original URLs and original capture dates in the author archive.

edition, 1932–1935. The established [original-work basis](../issue-99/source-context.json)
and retained [L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941)

for instalments applies to each element. DAF8 import notices and bibliographic
references are distinguished, with original credit where applicable. The wiki

identify the original *Dictionnaire de la langue française*, 1872–1877.
The retained [BnF record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb30824717s)

[original-work research](../../d2-resource-admission-research.md) records
[L123-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278937),
life plus seventy years, giving conservative ordinary expiry **1952-01-01**.
This is original-work credit where applicable, not sentence-authorship proof.
No modern XMLittré/ARTFL transcription is used.
