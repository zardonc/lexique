**All 200 fields, 189 selected source frames and 47 source contexts pass
separate independent rights review at the selected boundaries.** The
[field decisions](field-review.json), [rights ledger](rights.json) and
[source context](source-context.json) record the reviewer and reasons.
The [authored narrative](selected-rights-author-proposal.md) is retained;
article-wide licences and author conclusions were not used as automatic
selected-span acceptance.

## Contribution and adaptation route

Fixed Wiktionnaire copyright-policy revision **38903168**, retained Wikimedia
Terms of Use section **7** and the [complete CC BY-SA 4.0 legal text](COPYING),
sections 2(a), 3(a), 3(b) and 4, allow commercial sharing/adaptation with source
and author attribution, licence identification, modification notices,
ShareAlike and no additional restrictions. The policy also distinguishes
external material retaining separate rights. Fixed DAF8 import-template revision
**22113837** says all or part may have been imported and later modified; it does
not identify the author of a particular sentence.

All fields use selected lexical/phonetic contributions and scoped French
adaptations or newly written EN/ZH explanations. EN/ZH are not quotations from
external bilingual works. The [notices](NOTICES.md), legal text, fixed article
links, full histories, original-work credit and per-field modification records
accompany this reviewed evidence package. Adapted dictionary data remains
CC BY-SA 4.0; project code remains MIT.

All six contributor responses and their exact continuation requests were
independently checked. They yield **2,749 named contributor memberships across
47 articles**, with returned anonymous counts credited through complete linked
histories. This current registry can include editors after the fixed revision
and does not prove selected-sentence authorship.

## Original works

**DAF8** is recorded at 38 source contexts, including import notices and
bibliographic references distinguished in the ledger. The original Académie
française institutional eighth edition was published in volumes in **1932 and
1935**. The established original-work evidence and independently reviewed
[#119](../issue-119/rights-review.md) and
[#123 statutory/publication reading](../issue-123/selected-rights.md) support
the collective-work boundary under CPI L113-2. Retained
[CPI L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941)
provides seventy years from the January following publication, including
instalments; the conservative final-volume calculation is
**1936-01-01 + 70 years = 2006-01-01**. Original economic rights at that boundary
have expired. This does not admit modern enriched Académie digital prose.
Institution/work credit and modification information preserve attribution;
later wiki transcription/adaptation and Lexique explanations retain CC BY-SA 4.0.

**Littré** is recorded at four source contexts: `torturer`, `chute`, `donne`
and `fièvre`. The retained
[BnF catalogue](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb30824717s) identifies
Émile Littré **1801–1881** and the original 1873–1874 edition. The established
original-work research and CPI L123-1 life-plus-seventy rule give conservative
ordinary expiry **1952-01-01**. This resolves the original nineteenth-century
work, with original credit retained. No modern XMLittré/ARTFL transcription
supplies fields.

Original-work eligibility is separate from selected-sentence provenance.
Each original layer passes only its original-work boundary; page import or
reference markers alone do not establish sentence authorship. Current
contributor/adaptation obligations remain. Eight source contexts have no
recorded DAF8/Littré original layer.
