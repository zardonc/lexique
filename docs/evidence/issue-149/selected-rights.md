[complete legal text](COPYING), sections 2(a), 3(a), 3(b) and 4, permits sharing
and adaptation, including commercial use, with attribution, source and licence
identification, modification notices, ShareAlike and no additional restrictions.
Project source code remains MIT.

Retained Wikimedia Terms section 7 supplies the article/history attribution
route and preserves imported material's separate terms. French copyright-policy
revision 38903168 and DAF8-template revision 22113837 distinguish wiki
contributions from external works. Their API, raw policy, legal-code and Terms
captures were copied byte for byte from the #145 author archive; actual paths

remain identical to the retained origin copies.

## Original works and selected boundaries

Twenty-six captured articles declare DAF8 imports. Original credit remains to
Académie française, Dictionnaire de l’Académie française, eighth edition,
1932–1935. The established [#129 original-work evidence](../issue-129/selected-rights.md)
records the institutional collective-work basis under
[CPI L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941/2026-02-20).
Using final publication in 1935 gives 1936-01-01 plus seventy years:
**2006-01-01**. Original credit and modification notices remain; later wiki
transcription and Lexique adaptations retain CC BY-SA 4.0. That historical
original-work review does not review any new #149 wording. Import markers and
contributor totals do not prove selected-sentence authorship.

The CFC cartographic definition in `cadre`, line 103, is excluded. The selected
picture-frame definition is line 14; neither fields nor supporting spans use
the CFC definition. Bibliographic references, including external dictionary
references, remain context rather than general redistribution permission.
No quoted literary/news example, image, audio, etymology or other-language
section supplies a learner field.

`massacre` line 11 points to `massacrer`; its separately captured French verb
definition, line 7, supports the explicit people-killing explanation. That
support admits no extra verb group. `lesbien` line 24 selects the contemporary

of Canada reference metadata and neighbouring example prose are excluded from

## Construction and spelling

`sien/PRO:pos` retains the exact frozen identity. Fixed `le sien` lines 5–17
provide the masculine singular possessive pronoun, third-person singular
possessor and the agreement forms. The article's etymological component link,
line 3, and `sien` lines 8–10 establish the spelling relationship. The bare
adjective is an old use and supplies no selected meaning. The pronoun replaces
a masculine singular noun; its possessor can be male or female. `/sjɛ̃/` isolates
the recorded component in `/lə sjɛ̃/`; it does not claim the entire phrase is
pronounced without the article. No runtime alias is proposed.

`onze/ADJ:num` selects the preposed cardinal numeral, not the postposed ordinal
or noun. The selected portion of line 41 records traditional absence of liaison
and usual absence of elision, with the stated `de`/`que` exceptions. Embedded
reference quotations are outside that supporting excerpt. `zut/ONO` maps to
the actual French interjection frame. `surpris` and `endormi` select explicit
adjective frames, rather than substituting their verb participles.

`casse` selects feminine breakage, not masculine slang burglary. `condamné`
selects the person noun with the source's serious afflictive/infaming penalty
scope; that historical legal wording is not broadened to every minor conviction.
`lady` selects the title noun with its English social scope and a recorded
French pronunciation. `boss` selects the familiar French chief/employer noun.
`user` is the direct transitive wear-through-use verb; `adapter` selects the
pronominal adjustment to circumstances. The readable proposals retain these
and all other selected and excluded uses in [review-candidates.md](review-candidates.md).

Each IPA field selects one recorded applicable isolated variant. Every new
English and Chinese field records its selected French source basis and new
Lexique wording. None inherits independent quality review from a previous batch.

## Attribution and delivery

All 53 captured source articles have complete paginated named-contributor
registries, returned anonymous counts and linked full histories in
[source-context.json](source-context.json). Twelve retained API parts form seven
complete chains. A current registry can extend beyond the fixed revision; it
credits the article rather than identifying a selected sentence's author.

[NOTICES.md](NOTICES.md), legal text, source/history links, original-work credit,
modifications and selected excerpts accompany the proposals. Raw full captures
and owned helpers remain under the actual archive paths in
[source-captures.json](source-captures.json). The coordinator verifies independent
admission, actual combined source materials and notices, conversion and native
lookup/save/reopen. No field is admitted by this author package.
