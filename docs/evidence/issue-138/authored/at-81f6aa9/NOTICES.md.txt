# Source and modification notices for #138

The 50 exact groups and their frequency strings derive from Boris New and
Christophe Pallier’s [Lexique 3.83](https://chrplr.github.io/openlexicon/datasets-info/Lexique383/README-Lexique.html),
CC BY-SA 4.0, through the unchanged [scope manifest](../../../resources/core-pilot-79/scope-manifest.csv).
The denominator remains 4,087; frequency does not supply CEFR.

Wiktionnaire contributors supply the fixed French lexical and phonetic basis.
Lexique contributors supply sense selection, French adaptations and newly written
English/Chinese explanations, proposed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en).
The [complete legal text](COPYING), fixed revisions, histories and paginated
named/anonymous credits in [source context](source-context.json) accompany the
proposals. Registry credits are article credits, not selected-sentence authorship.
Project source code remains MIT. Independent review and admission are pending.

## Modifications

One selected sense per exact word/POS group; markup removed and selected use,
register and construction retained. English/Chinese are new scoped explanations,
not upstream bilingual quotations. IPA is one recorded isolated variant with
slashes added; other variants remain in the fixed captures. No audio, media,
literary/news quotations, etymologies or whole dictionaries are learner content.

`u/NOM` retains the attested lowercase French masculine invariant letter name,
pronounced /y/. `aïe/ONO` is the pain cry; `halte/ONO` is the specifically soldiers
stopping command with h aspiré. These bindings introduce no lookup alias.
