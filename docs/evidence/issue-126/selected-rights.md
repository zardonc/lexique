# Issue #126 selected-rights proposals

Each of the 200 proposed fields binds a fixed revision and exact source line in [rights.json](rights.json). The file enumerates the selected contribution layer, imported/original-work scope, article markers, attribution, modification and pending independent decision. French definitions are scoped adaptations; English and Chinese are newly authored explanations of those same uses, not upstream bilingual quotations.

The fixed Wiktionnaire textual contribution and Lexique adaptation layers use [CC BY-SA 4.0](COPYING). The retained Wikimedia Terms of Use section 7 permits attribution through article/history links while preserving imported content obligations. All 49 source contributor registries and public revision histories have complete API continuation chains in [source-captures.json](source-captures.json). Public editor identities and counts are retained; unavailable suppressed identities are not invented.

Académie eighth-edition original collective prose (1932–1935) uses the established [CPI L123-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278941/2026-02-20) basis, conservatively expired 2006-01-01, as recorded in [issue #119 rights review](../issue-119/rights-review.md). The referenced 1762 and 1798 collective editions also precede that economic-rights term. Émile Littré’s original nineteenth-century prose has a separate life-plus-seventy basis under [CPI L123-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278937), as recorded in [issue #122 selected-rights evidence](../issue-122/selected-rights.md). Modern publisher editorial material and XMLittré markup are outside those original-work scopes.

Article import notices mean all or part was imported. Bibliographies name works. Neither alone proves which person authored a selected sentence. The proposal credits all identified eligible historical-original layers conservatively and retains the exact markers for the separate reviewer; it does not assign current Wiki wording wholesale to an original author. Isolated IPA notation uses no original dictionary explanatory prose.
