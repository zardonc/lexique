## Source boundaries

flamme IPA /flam/ is the isolated variant in fr-rég at line 23. The 1871 pronunciation quotation and TLFi citations at lines 28–29 are not selected fields or construction support.
demi-heure definition line 8 and IPA line 7 are independent of the DAF9 etymological dating at line 3; DAF9 editorial prose is not selected.
exagérer definition line 7 is selected; the separately Littré-attributed definition at line 17 is not selected.
f definition line 39 and the letter-name /ɛf/ at line 38 are French, not translingual or a franc abbreviation. Short unattributed noun examples at lines 40–42 supply gender and noun-use evidence only.
méfier is bound to the explicit pronominal-only header at line 6 and person-distrust definition at line 7; no standalone verb or runtime alias is proposed.

All selected IPA variants are recorded, not generated. The source histories and contributor registries are fully paginated in [source-captures.json](source-captures.json). [source-excerpts.json](source-excerpts.json) retains exact lines and grammatical frames. [field-review.json](field-review.json) separates author checks from independent decisions. No alias or annotation is proposed.
